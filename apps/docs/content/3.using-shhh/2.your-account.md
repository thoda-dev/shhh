## Dashboard

`/dashboard` lists the pastes you created, with their status — active, expired or exhausted — and
lets you delete any of them early.

::note
The list shows metadata only: kind, size, dates, counters. Titles and previews are impossible by
design, since filenames and contents are encrypted with a key the server never had. A file shows as
a generic badge with its size.
::

## Two-factor authentication

`/account` enrols a TOTP authenticator. Scan the QR code, or type the secret manually, then confirm
with a code — **2FA is only activated once you prove your authenticator works**, so a bad setup
cannot lock you out.

Ten single-use backup codes are shown at enrolment. They are shown once. You can generate a fresh
set at any time, which invalidates the previous ones.

If your instance enforces 2FA, you are redirected to `/account` until you enrol, and the option to
turn it off is hidden.

## Changing your email

Requires the instance to have a mail provider. The confirmation link is sent to your **current**
address, not the new one — that is what prevents somebody holding a stolen session from quietly
moving your account to an address they control.

Your address stays unchanged until that link is used.

## Deleting your account

`/account` has a deletion section. It asks for your password and for you to retype your own email
address, then permanently deletes:

- your account, sessions and two-factor enrolment,
- every paste you created and their files,
- your statistics and the email recipient records of your shares.

Links you had already shared stop working immediately. Entries you left in the administrative audit
log are kept but anonymised.

::warning
This cannot be undone, and there is no recovery. The super admin account cannot delete itself —
transfer the role to somebody else first.
::
